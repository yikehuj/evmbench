# XLoot staking redemption

The repository contains the verified `Staking` contract and deterministic token/NFT fixtures. The Scenario deploys and seeds the staking contract; use its getters to locate the staking and xLoot addresses. Your submission is a sequence of transactions from the funded wallet. You must exploit the redemption accounting and leave positive ETH profit in your wallet while reducing the staking contract's ETH balance. Do not replace the verified contract, deploy a competing system, or mint tokens yourself. Submit only transactions; the grader checks on-chain balances and rejects empty or reverted submissions.
